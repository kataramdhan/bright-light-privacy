Privacy Policy

Bright Light – LED Flashlight (Android) · Developer: Kataramdhan
Effective date: November 15, 2026

Summary. Bright Light has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own. The app does not collect, store or share your personal information itself. The camera is used only for a live Magnifier preview — nothing is photographed, recorded, saved or uploaded. The free version shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob, which may collect certain device data as described below.

1. Who we are

Bright Light – LED Flashlight ("the app") is developed and published by Kataramdhan ("we", "us"). If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at lordramdhan@gmail.com.

2. Information the app handles

FeatureWhat happens
LED flashlight, SOS, Strobe, Auto-Off TimerControls your device's flash. No data is collected.
Magnifier (camera)Shows a live preview from the rear camera on your screen only. No photos or videos are captured, saved, or sent anywhere. Camera permission is requested only when you open Magnifier, and you can deny or revoke it at any time in Android settings.
CompassReads the device's magnetic and motion sensors to show a heading. The app does not request or use location (GPS) permission.
White Screen / Color LightTemporarily changes this app's screen brightness and keeps the screen on while in use. Your system brightness setting is not changed.
SettingsYour preferences (light brightness, color, strobe speed, safety-warning acknowledgment) are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.

We do not operate any backend, analytics, or crash-reporting service of our own, and we do not sell personal information.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays banner ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • Device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (e.g. device model, OS version, app version, language)
  • Ad interactions (e.g. ad views and taps) and diagnostic data

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us, and is transmitted in encrypted form. Depending on your choices and region, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Your choices and consent

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch the app shows a consent form provided by Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choices later in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown where required).
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording varies by device).
  • Permissions: you can revoke the camera permission at any time in Android settings; all other features keep working.
  • Offline use: all light, compass and magnifier features work without an internet connection. Ads require internet.

5. Data retention and deletion

We do not hold any of your personal data. Preferences stored on your device are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Data collected by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's policies; you can manage it through your Google account and device ad settings.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help address it.

7. Security

Because the app does not send personal information to us, there is no data on our side to breach. Communication between the ads SDK and Google uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

8. Safety notice

SOS is a visual signal only and does not contact emergency services. Strobe produces flashing light that may affect people with photosensitive epilepsy; a warning is shown before first use.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if the app's features or third-party services change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
